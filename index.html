Questions, answered

Before you call

What areas does Roque General Construction serve? +

We are based in Bend, Oregon and serve all of Central Oregon, including Redmond, Sisters, Tumalo, Terrebonne, Prineville, La Pine, Sunriver, Powell Butte, Madras and Crooked River Ranch. If you are close by, just call — we will confirm availability the same day.

Are you licensed, bonded and insured? +

Yes. Roque General Construction LLC is a licensed, bonded and insured Oregon contractor. We are happy to share our license number and certificate of insurance before any work begins.

Do you really offer free estimates? +

Yes — every estimate is 100% free with no obligation. Send a few photos through our quote form or request an on-site visit, and you will get a clear, itemized quote.

Can you handle several trades on the same project? +

That is exactly what we do best. With siding, framing, roofing repairs, demolition and junk removal under one roof, you get one crew, one schedule and one point of contact instead of juggling multiple contractors.

How soon can you start my project? +

Most estimates are returned within 24 to 48 hours. Scheduling depends on the season and project size, but we always give you a realistic start date up front — and we show up when we say we will.
